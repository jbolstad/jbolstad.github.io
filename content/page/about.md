<p style="text-align:justify; margin-top=24px">I am Professor of Political Science at the University of Oslo. My research and teaching interests generally lie within the fields of political behavior/ comparative politics and quantitative research methods. I have previously been Post-Doctoral Fellow at the University of Oslo, and worked as a Researcher and Lecturer at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology (ETH) in Zurich. I have also been a Fulbright Fellow at Harvard University, and a PhD Fellow of the Research Council of Norway, while earning my PhD at the European University Institute (EUI) in Florence, Italy. Before that, I obtained an MSc in International Political Economy from the London School of Economics (LSE). </p>

I am an alumnus of the <a href="https://akademietforyngreforskere.no" target="_blank"> Young Academy of Norway</a> (AYF), which was founded in 2015 at the initiative of the Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters. A presentation of my work and my thoughts on academia can be found on AYF's webpage, <a href="https://akademietforyngreforskere.no/medlem/jorgen-bolstad/" target="_blank">here</a>, while press coverage of the announcement of the membership can be found <a href="https://khrono.no/akademiet-for-yngre-forskere-ayf-bolstad/12-nye-yngre-forskarar-i-akademiet/411481" target="_blank">here</a> (both links are in Norwegian).

<p style="text-align:justify">I am using this page to share some posts on Bayesian methods. My posts will typically use R to illustrate statistical concepts and manually implement relevant procedures step by step. An advantage of this approach is that the codes will be self-sufficient and rarely require installing any packages. I will also provide codes to reproduce simplified versions of the plots I report, using only R base graphics. Finally, please note that my goal is not to provide computationally optimal codes for Bayesian analysis &ndash; for that you could just use <a href="http://www.mc-stan.org" target="_blank">Stan</a>. My goal is rather to provide hands-on illustrations that complement the abstract equations you will find in textbooks.</p>

### Media appearances and outreach

I have appeared regularly on Norwegian TV and radio, and in national newspapers. One of my most visible contributions to the non-academic world is my other webpage, <a href="https://www.estimite.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Estimite.com</a>. You can also find me on <a href="https://twitter.com/boelstad" target="_blank" rel="noopener">twitter</a>.

### Ongoing projects

<p style="text-align:justify">I am currently working on a number of projects in the fields of political behavior, political responsiveness, and quantitative methodology. Many of these projects entail developing new Bayesian models to address the topic at hand.</p>
